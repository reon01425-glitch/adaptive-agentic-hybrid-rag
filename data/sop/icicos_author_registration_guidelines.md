# ICICoS Author Guidelines and Registration Policy

## 1. Overview and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) governs all author registration, fee payment, and paper presentation activities for the International Conference on Information and Computer Systems (ICICoS). Adherence to these guidelines is mandatory for all accepted paper authors and conference participants to guarantee publication in IEEE Xplore.

## 2. Paper Registration and Fee Schedules
All accepted papers must be accompanied by at least one full author registration at the appropriate rate by the designated deadline. A single registration covers the presentation and publication of exactly one accepted paper.

### 2.1 Fee Category Matrix
| Category | Rate (Indonesian Rupiah / IDR) | International Rate (USD) | Eligibility Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Regular Author (Non-IEEE Member) | IDR 3,500,000 | USD 250 | General academic or professional authors |
| IEEE Member Author | IDR 3,000,000 | USD 200 | Valid IEEE Member Card (KTA IEEE) upload required |
| Student Author (Undergraduate / Graduate) | IDR 2,500,000 | USD 175 | Valid Student Identity Card (KTM) required; must be first author |
| Additional Paper (Same First Author) | IDR 2,000,000 | USD 150 | Maximum 1 additional paper per primary registration |
| Non-Author Attendee / Participant | IDR 1,500,000 | USD 100 | Includes access to all sessions and certificate |

### 2.2 Page Limit and Extra Page Surcharges
The standard registration fee includes manuscript publication up to six (6) pages in standard IEEE conference two-column format. 
- Maximum page limit: Eight (8) pages total.
- Extra page surcharge: IDR 300,000 (USD 25) per page for each page beyond six pages (maximum 2 extra pages permitted).
- Manuscripts exceeding eight pages will be automatically rejected from IEEE Xplore submission.

## 3. Mandatory Registration Attachments Checklist
Authors must compile and upload the following five mandatory attachments during the conference registration step:
1. **Camera-Ready Manuscript**: Final PDF compiled strictly according to the IEEE conference template.
2. **IEEE PDF eXpress Compliance Certificate**: Confirmation email PDF proving that the camera-ready manuscript successfully passed IEEE PDF eXpress validation with Conference ID `61234X`.
3. **Signed IEEE Electronic Copyright Form (eCF)**: Completed digital copyright transfer form submitted via EDAS.
4. **Official Bank Transfer Payment Receipt**: Valid bank transfer slip or transaction proof to the official conference Virtual Account (VA).
5. **Student Identification Card (KTM)**: Mandatory only if claiming the Student Author discount tier.

## 4. Payment Submission and Bank Virtual Account Protocol
1. Payment must be conducted exclusively via the designated University Bank Mandiri Virtual Account (VA) issued per paper ID. Cash payments or personal bank transfers to individual committee members are strictly prohibited.
2. Payment transfer proof must clearly indicate: Paper ID, First Author Name, Transaction Timestamp, and Exact Amount Paid.
3. Once payment is transferred, the author must submit the transaction slip to the ICICoS portal within three (3) calendar days of the registration deadline.

## 5. Letter of Acceptance (LoA) and Presentation Schedule Issuance
1. The Letter of Acceptance (LoA) and official payment voucher will only be issued by the Secretariat after the Conference Treasurer (Bendahara) verifies and approves the payment slip against bank account mutations.
2. Under no circumstances may an author receive an LoA prior to formal Treasurer validation.
3. Upon LoA issuance, the Secretariat includes the paper in the official conference parallel presentation schedule and proceedings catalogue.
