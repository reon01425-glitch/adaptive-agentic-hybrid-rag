# ICICoS Financial Accounting, Payment Verification, and Honoraria SOP

## 1. Scope and Organizational Authority
This document defines the financial auditing standards, payment validation rules, and honorarium disbursement mechanisms for ICICoS. Financial operations are administered by the Conference Treasurer (Bendahara) under the oversight of the General Chair and Faculty Finance Division.

## 2. Payment Receipt Verification and Audit Rules
### 2.1 Treasurer Audit Mandate
All submitted payment receipts must undergo rigorous validation by the Conference Treasurer against daily bank Virtual Account (VA) transaction mutation records.
1. The Treasurer checks: (a) exact transfer amount matching the registration tier, (b) paper ID reference in transaction remarks, (c) payment timestamp prior to deadline.
2. Verification turnaround time: within two (2) working days of slip upload.

### 2.2 Payment Discrepancy and Rejection Protocol (XOR Gateway)
Upon auditing the payment receipt, the Treasurer must execute one of two mutually exclusive actions:
- **Condition [Valid Payment Receipt]**: If the transfer amount, paper ID, and bank mutation match perfectly, the Treasurer marks the transaction as "Verified / Valid" in the conference management database. This automatically triggers a notification to the Secretariat to issue the Letter of Acceptance (LoA) and payment receipt.
- **Condition [Invalid Payment Receipt / Underpayment / Discrepancy]**: If there is an underpayment, illegible slip, invalid transaction number, or missing paper ID, the Treasurer marks the status as "Payment Discrepancy / Rejected". The Treasurer immediately issues an official Payment Rejection Notice to the author via email.
  - The author is granted a mandatory three (3) working day grace period to upload a corrected transaction slip or pay the remaining balance.
  - During this period, NO Letter of Acceptance (LoA) or invoice may be issued.
  - If the author fails to remediate the discrepancy within three working days, the paper registration is officially cancelled.

## 3. Speaker and Reviewer Honoraria Guidelines (Surat Pertanggungjawaban / SPJ)
### 3.1 Mandatory SPJ Attachment Checklist
In compliance with Indonesian Ministry of Finance and University financial governance regulations, all honorarium disbursements for keynote speakers, invited session chairs, and peer reviewers require a complete Surat Pertanggungjawaban (SPJ) voucher portfolio.
Mandatory SPJ attachments include:
1. **Official Committee Assignment Decree (SK Kepanitiaan)**: Dean or Rector decree appointing the recipient.
2. **Invitation Letter and Confirmation of Acceptance**: Signed formal invitation and participant confirmation.
3. **Activity Report and Attendance Verification**: Presentation slide deck summary, session log, or reviewer evaluation sheet tally.
4. **Taxpayer Identification Number (NPWP)**: Copy of NPWP card (or Indonesian NIK) for income tax withholding (PPh Pasal 21: 5% for NPWP holders, 6% for non-NPWP holders).
5. **Signed Honorarium Receipt Voucher (Kuitansi Honorarium)**: Signed receipt slip indicating gross honorarium, tax deduction, and net payment.
6. **Recipient Bank Account Passbook Copy**: Legible copy showing account number, account holder name, and bank branch.

### 3.2 Honoraria Disbursement Process
1. Recipient submits completed SPJ package to Conference Treasurer.
2. Treasurer audits attachment completeness and calculates withholding tax.
3. General Chair signs the payment requisition voucher.
4. University Finance executes direct electronic bank transfer to the recipient's personal account. Cash disbursement is strictly prohibited.

## 4. Refund and Cancellation Policy
1. Registration fees are non-refundable once an accepted paper has been registered, as editorial processing and conference resource reservation commence immediately.
2. In exceptional cases of duplicate payments caused by banking system errors, the author must submit a formal refund request along with both transaction slips within seven (7) calendar days. Approved refunds are subject to an administrative processing fee of 15%.
