# ICICoS Camera-Ready Preparation and IEEE Xplore Ingestion SOP

## 1. Scope and Technical Standards
This SOP governs the preparation, verification, and final submission of camera-ready manuscripts for ICICoS to ensure compliance with IEEE Xplore digital library standards.

## 2. IEEE PDF eXpress Validation
### 2.1 Certification Requirement
Every camera-ready manuscript must be validated through the official IEEE PDF eXpress online system prior to submission.
1. Conference ID: `61234X`.
2. Authors must create an IEEE PDF eXpress account, upload their source files or preliminary PDF, and obtain a certified PDF directly from the tool.
3. The confirmation email with the validation stamp must be retained and submitted alongside the final paper.
4. Uncertified PDFs or manuscripts failing PDF eXpress validation will be rejected by the IEEE packaging pipeline.

## 3. IEEE Copyright Transfer Protocol
### 3.1 Electronic Copyright Form (eCF)
1. In accordance with IEEE policy, authors must transfer copyright to IEEE prior to publication.
2. The primary corresponding author must complete the IEEE Electronic Copyright Form (eCF) wizard integrated into the EDAS conference management system.
3. The generated IEEE copyright notice must be placed on the bottom left corner of the first page of the camera-ready manuscript:
   - For papers where all authors are employed by the US government: `U.S. Government work not protected by U.S. copyright`
   - For papers where all authors are employed by a Crown government: `979-8-3503-XXXX-X/26/$31.00 ©2026 Crown`
   - For papers where all authors are employed by the European Union: `979-8-3503-XXXX-X/26/$31.00 ©2026 European Union`
   - For all other papers: `979-8-3503-XXXX-X/26/$31.00 ©2026 IEEE`

## 4. Final Verification and Publication Chair Audit (XOR Gateway)
Upon receiving the camera-ready package, the Publication Chair audits the submission:
- **Condition [Compliant Camera-Ready Package]**:
  1. Valid IEEE PDF eXpress certificate verified.
  2. Correct IEEE copyright stamp present on page 1.
  3. No page numbers, headers, or footers added.
  4. Registered author list matches submitted manuscript.
  5. Action: Publication Chair approves paper for inclusion in the IEEE Conference Proceedings and signs off on the final submission package to IEEE Xplore.
- **Condition [Format Defect / Non-Compliant Package]**:
  1. Missing copyright stamp, broken fonts, margin violations, or page limit exceeded.
  2. Action: Publication Chair issues a Format Defect Notice to the author. The author must correct and re-upload the manuscript within 48 hours. Failure to correct leads to exclusion from IEEE Xplore.

## 5. Post-Conference IEEE Xplore Ingestion
1. The Conference Chair and Publication Chair prepare the complete IEEE conference proceedings package, including cataloging data and session logs.
2. The package is delivered to IEEE Conference Operations within four (4) weeks post-conference.
3. Published papers typically appear in IEEE Xplore within 6 to 10 weeks following formal ingestion approval.
