# ICICoS Peer Review and Editorial Governance SOP

## 1. Scope and Editorial Policy
This SOP establishes the quality standards, peer review lifecycle, and decision thresholds for papers submitted to ICICoS. All submissions must represent original, unpublished research and undergo rigorous double-blind peer review.

## 2. Desk Review and Plagiarism Screening
### 2.1 Technical Program Committee (TPC) Desk Review
Upon paper submission by the author, the TPC Chair conducts an initial desk evaluation:
1. **Formatting Check**: Adherence to standard IEEE two-column manuscript layout and 6-page baseline.
2. **Double-Blind Anonymity**: Complete removal of author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, and self-identifying citations. Violations result in immediate return to author for de-anonymization within 24 hours.
3. **Plagiarism and Similarity Screening**: Checked using Turnitin and IEEE CrossCheck.
   - Overall similarity score must not exceed 20%.
   - Similarity with any single external source must not exceed 5%.
   - Papers exceeding 20% overall similarity or 5% single-source overlap are summarily rejected without peer review.

## 3. Double-Blind Peer Review Execution
### 3.1 Reviewer Assignment
1. Each desk-approved manuscript is assigned to at least three (3) independent reviewers selected based on subject matter expertise and absence of conflicts of interest.
2. Reviewers have three (3) weeks to complete evaluations using the standard ICICoS 5-point evaluation rubric.

### 3.2 Scoring Rubric and Decision Thresholds (XOR Gateway)
Reviewers score papers across five dimensions (Originality, Technical Soundness, Methodology, Clarity, Relevance) on a 1.0 to 5.0 scale. The overall weighted average score determines the TPC decision via an exclusive gateway:
- **Branch [Score >= 3.5: Accept]**: The paper meets international quality standards. The TPC Chair issues an official Acceptance Notification directing the author to prepare camera-ready files and complete author registration.
- **Branch [Score 2.80 - 3.49: Major Revision Required]**: The paper has merit but requires significant technical or experimental amendments. The author is given ten (10) calendar days to submit a revised manuscript and detailed response to reviewers. The revised manuscript is re-evaluated by the TPC Chair before final acceptance.
- **Branch [Score < 2.80: Reject]**: The paper fails to meet technical criteria or displays critical methodological flaws. The TPC Chair issues an official Rejection Notification with consolidated reviewer feedback. Decisions are final.

## 4. Rebuttal and Author Inquiries
1. Authors may submit a factual rebuttal within five (5) days only if reviewer reports contain demonstrable factual errors regarding the manuscript contents.
2. Rebuttals are reviewed by the TPC Executive Committee; decisions of the committee are irrevocable.
